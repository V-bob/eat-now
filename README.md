# Eat Now

Where can I eat right now that's close and fits my diet? Open places first, then closest, with
dining hall menus and allergen filters for the University of Illinois, and restaurants in every US
and Canadian city of 20,000+ people.

**Open it:** https://v-bob.github.io/eat-now/

This repository holds the published website. It's rebuilt every morning so the University of
Illinois dining menus always cover the next week (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`).

## Where the data comes from

- Restaurants, cafés, and their hours: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Hours may be out of date.
- Dining hall menus and allergen tags: University of Illinois Housing.
- US cities: US Census Bureau. Canadian cities: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).
- Buses: Champaign-Urbana MTD. Walking and bike routes: routing.openstreetmap.de (FOSSGIS).
